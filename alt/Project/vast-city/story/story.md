# Vast Open: Gilded Blade

*vast open world city with districts*

## Lore
Before the chalice, the forgotten kept the Hollow. Then came the gilded age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Hollow.

You arrive with nothing but scroll and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Keep)

- Reach Keep and guard the blade before rival seekers do.
- Recover the gilded blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- unravel three blades hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Vale)

- An NPC at Vale trades passage for the moonlit chalice.
- Survive the gauntlet past Vale; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Vale and unravel the prophecy before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Crossing)

- Recover the thornbound chalice; it is the key to the next threshold.
- guard three chalices hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Crossing trades passage for the thornbound warden.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, verdant

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Keep)

- Survive the gauntlet past Keep; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Keep and restore the blade before rival seekers do.
- Recover the moonlit briar; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen
