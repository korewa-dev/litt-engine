# Neon Rooftop: Verdant Warden

*neon rooftop escape*

## Lore
Before the briar, the moonlit kept the Vale. Then came the moonlit age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Crossing.

You arrive with nothing but armor and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Keep)

- Reach Keep and seek the crown before rival seekers do.
- Recover the forgotten blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Wyrm of Vale
