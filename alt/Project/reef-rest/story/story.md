# Cozy Island: Thornbound Riddle

*cozy island farming tale, small game*

## Lore
Before the crown, the moonlit kept the Hollow. Then came the verdant age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Throne.

You arrive with nothing but weapon and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Vale)

- Reach Vale and unravel the prophecy before rival seekers do.
- Recover the ancient grove; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen
