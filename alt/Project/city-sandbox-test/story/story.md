# Open World: Ancient Blade

*open world exploration*

## Lore
Before the briar, the gilded kept the Throne. Then came the ancient age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Hollow.

You arrive with nothing but trinket and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Wood)

- Reach Wood and restore the blade before rival seekers do.
- Recover the wyrm-sung blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- restore three riddles hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Keep)

- An NPC at Keep trades passage for the gilded chalice.
- Survive the gauntlet past Keep; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Keep and guard the blade before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Wyrm of Vale

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Crossing)

- Recover the ancient warden; it is the key to the next threshold.
- guard three chalices hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Crossing trades passage for the ancient prophecy.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper, gilded

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Keep)

- Survive the gauntlet past Keep; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Keep and unravel the blade before rival seekers do.
- Recover the verdant warden; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen
