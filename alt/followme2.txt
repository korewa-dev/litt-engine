#########################################################################
# SECTION 1: GAME IDENTITY
#########################################################################

**Title:** "Concrete Echoes"
**Genre:** Open-world, third-person action-adventure, crime, sandbox
**Setting:** "Greyport City" — coastal metropolis, 2010s-2020s
**Protagonist:** Elias Ward
**Tone:** Gritty, overcast, grounded realism (NOT over-the-top)
**Inspiration:** GTA IV (NOT V) — weighty driving, street-level drama, moral choices

**Core Fantasy:**
Player is a newcomer to a harsh city, climbing from the bottom of the
criminal underworld while wrestling with loyalty, morality, and survival.

**Core Pillars:**
1. Grounded driving and physics — heavy, weighty vehicles
2. Street-level combat — cover-based shooting, melee, takedowns
3. Narrative missions — branching choices, character-driven dialogue
4. Sandbox freedom — free-roam city, side activities, random encounters

#########################################################################
# SECTION 2: CITY LAYOUT — GREYPORT CITY
#########################################################################

**Overall Layout:**
- Single contiguous open world with streaming sectors
- Coastal city with multiple boroughs, bridges, docks, industrial zones
- Dense downtown skyline, elevated train lines, riverside promenade
- Size: Approximately 10km x 10km playable area
- Seamless loading via sector streaming (use litt_scene.h + litt_lod.h)

**Key Features:**
- Central bridge connecting Dockside and Midtown
- Elevated train line with stations and tracks
- City hall plaza with protest events and police presence
- Large stadium/arena for mid-game mission
- Derelict factory complex for climactic shootout
- Riverside promenade with pedestrians and side activities
- High-rise rooftop cluster for sniper and parkour missions
- Hidden underground tunnel system for smuggling and escape routes

**Interiors:**
- Safehouses (save, change clothes, store vehicles)
- Bars and diners (social hubs, minor side missions)
- Gang hideouts (combat-focused interiors)
- Small shops (basic economy, buying clothes, food, minor items)

**Terrain:**
- Heightmap-based terrain (use litt_terrain_system.h)
- Generate with: TerrainSystem::generate_heightmap(seed)
- Query heights: TerrainSystem::get_height_at(world_pos)
- Roads and sidewalks are part of the terrain mesh

# SECTION 3: DISTRICTS (4 MAJOR)
#########################################################################

### District A: Dockside Quarter
- **Features:** Cargo docks, warehouses, shipyard cranes, smuggling routes
- **Gameplay:** Early missions, low-level gangs, car theft, smuggling jobs
- **Landmarks:** Central cargo crane, shipping containers, dockmaster office
- **Atmosphere:** Industrial, salt water, seagulls, constant fog
- **Engine Use:** litt_particle_system.h (fog, smoke), litt_terrain_system.h (water)

### District B: Oldstone
- **Features:** Older brick buildings, narrow streets, small shops, tenements
- **Gameplay:** Street chases, gang turf wars, alley shootouts
- **Landmarks:** Tenement block, old church, underground market
- **Atmosphere:** Gritty, neon signs, graffiti, rain-slicked streets
- **Engine Use:** litt_ui_system.h (shop interiors), litt_bvh.h (narrow streets)

### District C: Midtown Heights
- **Features:** High-rise offices, corporate plazas, expensive apartments
- **Gameplay:** Heists, corporate espionage, high-speed chases on wide avenues
- **Landmarks:** Corporate tower, luxury mall, city park
- **Atmosphere:** Wealthy, glass buildings, clean streets, helicopters
- **Engine Use:** litt_lod.h (distant skyline), litt_gpu_software.h (glass reflections)

### District D: Ironbelt Industrial
- **Features:** Factories, rail yards, refineries, scrapyards
- **Gameplay:** Large-scale shootouts, vehicle-based missions, stealth infiltration
- **Landmarks:** Steel mill, rail yard, scrapyard, chemical plant
- **Atmosphere:** Smokestacks, fire, molten metal, workers
- **Engine Use:** litt_particle_system.h (fire, smoke), litt_collision.h (heavy objects)

#########################################################################
# SECTION 4: CHARACTER AND STORY
#########################################################################

### Main Characters

**Elias Ward (Player):**
- Reserved, observant, morally conflicted
- Background: Immigrant with troubled past, arriving in Greyport to escape old debts
- Motivation: Pay off debts, protect a small circle of friends/family
- Combat style: Practical, uses whatever is available, not a super-soldier

**Mara Ionescu (Early contact):**
- Streetwise fixer, sarcastic, pragmatic
- Introduces Elias to local jobs

**Darius Cole (Gang leader):**
- Charismatic but ruthless, runs a mid-level gang in Oldstone

**Detective Rowan Hale (Corrupt cop):**
- Uses Elias for dirty work, offers protection in exchange for favors

**Juno Vega (Tech-savvy ally):**
- Handles hacking, surveillance, and intel for high-level missions

**Lena Ward (Family tie):**
- Elias's younger sibling, non-criminal, represents moral anchor

### Factions
- **Dockside Crew:** Smugglers and dock workers turned criminals
- **Oldstone Kings:** Street gang controlling tenements and small businesses
- **Ironbelt Union:** Mix of workers and criminals in industrial zones
- **Greyport PD:** Police force with both honest and corrupt elements

### Story Arc (3 Acts)

**Act I: Arrival & Survival**
- Elias arrives in Greyport
- Introduced to Mara and Dockside Crew
- Early missions: debt collection, small-time smuggling, learning city layout
- Climax: A botched job that draws attention from Oldstone Kings and Greyport PD

**Act II: Climbing the Ladder**
- Elias works for Darius Cole and Detective Hale
- Missions: gang wars, police corruption, mid-level heists
- Player makes choices: side with gang vs. cop in certain missions
- Climax: Major betrayal or double-cross, forcing Elias to choose alliances

**Act III: Consequences & Resolution**
- City-wide tension between factions
- High-risk missions: industrial sabotage, large-scale heists, confrontations
- Multiple endings based on key choices:
  - Escape the city with family
  - Take over a faction
  - Cooperate with Hale to become a protected asset

# SECTION 5: CORE SYSTEMS (WITH ENGINE API MAPPING)
#########################################################################

### 5.1 Player Movement
**States:** Idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, jump, climb, vault, cover
**Cover System:** Snap to cover near walls, cars, low obstacles
**Parkour-lite:** Simple vaulting over low obstacles, climbing ladders, mantling ledges

**Engine API:**
- litt_physics.h — PhysicsBody for character controller
- litt_input.h — Input::key_down(), Input::mouse_position()
- litt_collision.h — CollisionSystem::aabb_collision() for wall detection
- litt_scene.h — SceneNode for position updates

### 5.2 Combat
**Weapons:** Pistols, SMGs, Shotguns, Assault Rifles, Melee (bat, pipe, knife)
**Aiming:** Over-the-shoulder with crosshair, optional soft lock-on
**Damage:** Health bar with partial regeneration, headshots deal extra damage
**Cover Combat:** Blind fire from cover, pop-out aiming from cover
**Melee:** Light and heavy attacks, contextual finishers when enemy is low

**Engine API:**
- litt_physics.h — PhysicsBody for bullets (raycasts)
- litt_collision.h — CollisionSystem::raycast() for hit detection
- litt_gpu_software.h — SoftwareRenderer::draw_line() for crosshair
- litt_particle_system.h — ParticleSystem for muzzle flash, blood
- litt_profiler.h — Profiler::begin_sample/end_sample for combat timing

### 5.3 Driving
**Vehicle Types:** Compact cars, sedans, muscle cars, vans, trucks, police cars, taxis
**Handling:** Weighty, slightly slippery, different traction and acceleration per class
**Damage:** Visual (dents, broken lights, smoke) + mechanical (handling, speed)
**Camera:** Chase camera behind vehicle, optional hood/bumper camera
**Traffic:** AI cars follow lanes, obey traffic lights loosely

**Engine API:**
- litt_physics.h — PhysicsBody for vehicle (mass, velocity, friction)
- litt_collision.h — CollisionSystem for vehicle-vehicle and vehicle-wall
- litt_scene.h — SceneNode for vehicle position/rotation
- litt_lod.h — LODSystem for distant vehicles
- litt_gpu_software.h — SoftwareRenderer::draw_triangle_3d() for vehicle rendering

### 5.4 Wanted System (0-5 Stars)
**1 Star:** Local patrol cars investigate
**2 Stars:** Multiple patrol cars, more aggressive
**3 Stars:** Roadblocks, more officers
**4 Stars:** SWAT-like units, heavy weapons
**5 Stars:** Helicopter support, extreme aggression
**Triggers:** Crimes witnessed by civilians/police, shooting, stealing cars, running over pedestrians
**Evasion:** Breaking line of sight and staying hidden for a duration

**Engine API:**
- litt_event.h — EventDispatcher for crime events
- litt_ai.h (via litt_scripting_vm.h) — ScriptVM for police AI logic
- litt_ui.h — UIManager for wanted level display
- litt_profiler.h — Profiler for tracking wanted level duration

### 5.5 Economy & Progression
**Currency:** Cash
**Income Sources:** Story missions, side jobs, robberies
**Spending:** Weapons/ammo, clothes, vehicle repairs/upgrades
**Progression:** Story missions unlock districts and contacts, reputation system

**Engine API:**
- litt_serialization.h — JSONSerializer for save/load
- litt_config.h — Settings for game balance values
- litt_json.h — lvj_parse() for world_state.json
- litt_ui.h — UIManager for shop menus

### 5.6 NPC AI
**Pedestrians:** Walking, standing, talking, using phones, reacting to violence
**Enemy AI:** Taking cover, flanking, retreating when injured, difficulty scaling
**Police AI:** Investigate, chase, roadblocks, helicopter at high wanted levels

**Engine API:**
- litt_scripting_vm.h — ScriptVM for behavior state machines
- litt_spatial_hash.h — SpatialHashGrid for NPC proximity queries
- litt_bvh.h — BVHBuilder for AI raycasting (line of sight)
- litt_event.h — EventDispatcher for NPC reactions

### 5.7 Mission System
**Mission Triggers:** Markers in the world
**Pre-mission Briefings:** Dialogue, short cutscenes
**Objectives:** Go to X, talk to Y, steal Z, chase, escort, defend, etc.
**Failure Conditions:** Player death, target death, losing target, etc.
**Checkpoints:** Within missions
**Rewards:** Cash, reputation, unlocks

**Mission State Machine:**
INACTIVE → ACTIVE → COMPLETED or FAILED

**Engine API:**
- litt_scripting_vm.h — ScriptVM for mission logic
- litt_event.h — EventDispatcher for mission events
- litt_ui.h — UIManager for mission objectives display
- litt_scene.h — SceneManager for mission scene switching
- litt_profiler.h — Profiler for mission timing

### 5.8 UI/HUD
**Top-left:** Player health bar
**Top-right:** Cash amount, wanted level stars
**Bottom-left:** Mini-map with player arrow, mission markers, police markers
**Bottom-right:** Current weapon icon, ammo count
**Center:** Contextual prompts (e.g., "Press [E] to enter vehicle")

**Engine API:**
- litt_ui.h — UIManager, UIButton, UIText, UISlider
- litt_gpu_software.h — SoftwareRenderer for 2D HUD drawing
- litt_input.h — Input for key binding

### 5.9 Audio
**Ambient:** Traffic, distant sirens, construction, crowd chatter, seagulls near docks
**Weather:** Rain with wet-surface sound effects, occasional thunder
**Interior Ambience:** Bar music, muffled conversations, TV chatter
**Music:** Moody, urban, low-key electronic and rock influences
**Voice & Dialogue:** Triggered lines, mission dialogue, police radio chatter

**Engine API:**
- litt_audio_wav.h — WindowsWaveOutAudio for WAV playback
- litt_event.h — EventDispatcher for audio triggers
- litt_profiler.h — Profiler for audio performance

### 5.10 Save/Load
**Save:** Player position, current mission/objective, cash, inventory, reputation
**Load:** Restore all above, reinitialize mission scripts and AI states

**Engine API:**
- litt_serialization.h — JSONSerializer for save/load
- litt_config.h — Settings for configuration
- litt_json.h — lvj_parse() for loading save files

# SECTION 6: MISSION STRUCTURE
#########################################################################

### Mission Types (15-20 Minimum)
1. **Chase missions** — On foot and in vehicles
2. **Escort missions** — Protect an ally from attackers
3. **Heist missions** — Multi-stage objectives (scouting, infiltration, escape)
4. **Assassination missions** — Stealth or loud approaches
5. **Defense missions** — Hold a location against waves of enemies
6. **Investigation missions** — Follow suspects, gather intel, avoid detection

### Branching Choices
- Help Darius or Hale
- Spare or kill a rival
- Protect family at the cost of betraying an ally
- These affect faction reputation, mission availability, and ending variations

### Example Mission Script (using litt_scripting_vm.h opcodes)
```
PUSH "Dockside Warehouse"   // Location
PUSH "Steal the cargo"     // Objective
CALL start_mission         // Begin mission
WAIT objective_complete    // Wait for player
CALL reward_cash           // Give reward
CALL next_mission          // Progress story
```
