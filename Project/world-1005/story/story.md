# Open World: Thornbound Warden

*open world exploration*

## Lore
Before the riddle, the ancient kept the Hollow. Then came the gilded age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Keep.

You arrive with nothing but weapon and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Hollow)

- Reach Hollow and unravel the chalice before rival seekers do.
- Recover the thornbound blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- seek three briars hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Throne)

- An NPC at Throne trades passage for the ancient chalice.
- Survive the gauntlet past Throne; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Throne and seek the riddle before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, wyrm-sung

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Hollow)

- Recover the ancient warden; it is the key to the next threshold.
- guard three blades hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Hollow trades passage for the ancient crown.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, ancient

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Hollow)

- Survive the gauntlet past Hollow; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Hollow and unravel the grove before rival seekers do.
- Recover the ancient chalice; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, verdant
