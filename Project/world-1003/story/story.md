# Open World: Gilded Grove

*open world exploration*

## Lore
Before the blade, the forgotten kept the Wood. Then came the ancient age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Hollow.

You arrive with nothing but consumable and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Crossing)

- Reach Crossing and unravel the chalice before rival seekers do.
- Recover the verdant briar; it is the key to the next threshold.
- guard three wardens hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Hollow)

- An NPC at Hollow trades passage for the forgotten riddle.
- Survive the gauntlet past Hollow; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Hollow and seek the warden before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Keep)

- Recover the wyrm-sung warden; it is the key to the next threshold.
- seek three riddles hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Keep trades passage for the verdant blade.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, moonlit

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Wood)

- Survive the gauntlet past Wood; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Wood and seek the crown before rival seekers do.
- Recover the gilded prophecy; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Wyrm of Vale
