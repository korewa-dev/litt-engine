# Dark Souls: Pale Sigil

*dark souls full game, big world with bosses and items*

## Lore
Before the relic, the silent kept the Bastion. Then came the silent age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Court.

You arrive with nothing but relic and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Garden)

- Reach Garden and kindle the cinder before rival seekers do.
- Recover the forsaken relic; it is the key to the next threshold.
- kindle three relics hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** Vow-Knight Aldric

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Parish)

- An NPC at Parish trades passage for the twilight pyre.
- Survive the gauntlet past Parish; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Parish and break the oath before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** Sister Cinder

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Bastion)

- Recover the unlit pyre; it is the key to the next threshold.
- break three relics hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Bastion trades passage for the embered ash.
- **Boss:** the Pale Warden

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Parish)

- Survive the gauntlet past Parish; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Parish and kindle the relic before rival seekers do.
- Recover the ruined cinder; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Ember-Eyed Hound
