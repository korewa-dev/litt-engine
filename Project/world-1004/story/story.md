# Open World: Ancient Warden

*open world exploration*

## Lore
Before the prophecy, the forgotten kept the Throne. Then came the moonlit age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Vale.

You arrive with nothing but scroll and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Vale)

- Reach Vale and guard the chalice before rival seekers do.
- Recover the verdant riddle; it is the key to the next threshold.
- guard three chalices hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Throne)

- An NPC at Throne trades passage for the forgotten briar.
- Survive the gauntlet past Throne; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Throne and unravel the grove before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Vale)

- Recover the thornbound blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- restore three prophecys hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Vale trades passage for the verdant prophecy.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper, verdant

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Throne)

- Survive the gauntlet past Throne; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Throne and seek the riddle before rival seekers do.
- Recover the forgotten grove; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien
