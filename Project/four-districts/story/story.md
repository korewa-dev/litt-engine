# Open World: Gilded Riddle

*open world city exploration four districts*

## Lore
Before the blade, the gilded kept the Hollow. Then came the wyrm-sung age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Keep.

You arrive with nothing but armor and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Vale)

- Reach Vale and guard the warden before rival seekers do.
- Recover the wyrm-sung chalice; it is the key to the next threshold.
- restore three briars hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Keep)

- An NPC at Keep trades passage for the thornbound prophecy.
- Survive the gauntlet past Keep; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Keep and guard the chalice before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Vale)

- Recover the moonlit grove; it is the key to the next threshold.
- restore three prophecys hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Vale trades passage for the moonlit blade.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen, ancient

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Wood)

- Survive the gauntlet past Wood; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Wood and restore the riddle before rival seekers do.
- Recover the verdant briar; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** High Warden Elucien, gilded
