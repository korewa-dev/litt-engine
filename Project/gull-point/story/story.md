# Misty Lighthouse: Forgotten Grove

*misty lighthouse keeper mystery, small game*

## Lore
Before the prophecy, the wyrm-sung kept the Crossing. Then came the thornbound age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Keep.

You arrive with nothing but trinket and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Wood)

- Reach Wood and unravel the briar before rival seekers do.
- Recover the ancient prophecy; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen
