# Open World: Moonlit Chalice

*open world exploration*

## Lore
Before the warden, the moonlit kept the Keep. Then came the gilded age: bells went quiet, names were spent, and something ancient began to wait beneath Hollow.

You arrive with nothing but weapon and a reason you refuse to say aloud.

## Act 1 - The Long Approach (Wood)

- Reach Wood and guard the blade before rival seekers do.
- Recover the thornbound crown; it is the key to the next threshold.
- unravel three wardens hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper

## Act 2 - What the Bells Buried (Wood)

- An NPC at Wood trades passage for the ancient prophecy.
- Survive the gauntlet past Wood; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Wood and restore the prophecy before rival seekers do.
- **Boss:** the Thorn Queen

## Act 3 - Ash Below, Sky Above (Wood)

- Recover the verdant blade; it is the key to the next threshold.
- unravel three blades hidden across the level - each one quiets a bell.
- An NPC at Wood trades passage for the gilded riddle.
- **Boss:** the Wyrm of Vale

## Act 4 - The Turning of the Vigil (Vale)

- Survive the gauntlet past Vale; the road narrows behind you.
- Reach Vale and seek the prophecy before rival seekers do.
- Recover the wyrm-sung grove; it is the key to the next threshold.
- **Boss:** the Gilded Usurper, thornbound
